# Payment image sources

The 63 PNG filenames follow README.md. Downloaded 2026-10-01. Original logos were converted to PNG, centered on square transparent canvases, and resized only to fit. Vectors are rendered at high resolution; raster sources are not enlarged to invent detail. Existing opaque brand backgrounds are retained. Source dimensions are listed below.

Cash on delivery and card payment use Lucide icons (ISC license; see LUCIDE-LICENSE.txt). Brand marks belong to their respective owners.

Legacy entries use archived branding where available. `pak-oman-microfinance-bank.png` uses the official LOLC successor logo; [LOLC explains the name change](https://www.lolc.com.pk/about-us/). The First MicroFinance Bank and FINCA retain their legacy logos as listed in README.md. Easypaisa and Easypaisa Bank share the same digital-bank logo.
